Screen share & casting

Google Home / Chromecast

  1. Open the Google Home app on this phone.
  2. Tap your Chromecast / Google TV device.
  3. Choose Cast my screen → Cast screen and accept.
  4. Come back to FairwayLink, tap the ⛶ full-screen button and turn the phone landscape.
  5. The simulator fills the TV; the PiP camera window stays on the phone so you can see your ball.

Samsung Smart View

  1. Swipe down the notification shade twice and find Smart View.
  2. Select your Samsung TV or projector.
  3. Set the TV to full screen if it shows a mirrored box.
  4. Tap ⛶ in FairwayLink for immersive mode (hides browser bars).
  5. Keep the phone in landscape for a 16:9 fill.

Tips for a clean picture

  • Casting adds 100–400 ms of latency — watch the phone for swing feedback, use the TV for the shot view.
  • Enable Hide UI when screen sharing in Settings for a cleaner broadcast.
  • Keep the phone plugged in; camera analysis + casting is power hungry.
  • Turn off auto-rotate lock if the picture stays portrait.
